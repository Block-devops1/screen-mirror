Before You Connect

One-time setup for your Honor X9a 5G → Chromebook

1
On your phone: Settings → About Phone, tap Build Number 7 times to unlock Developer Options.
2
Go to Settings → Developer Options and enable USB Debugging.
3
Plug your phone into the Chromebook via USB. When prompted on the phone, tap "Allow USB Debugging" and check "Always allow from this computer".
4
Open your video in VLC on the phone, then click CONNECT PHONE and select your Honor device.
