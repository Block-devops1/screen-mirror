Before You Connect

One-time setup for your Android → Chromebook

1
On your phone: Settings → About Phone, tap Build Number 7 times to unlock Developer Options.
2
Go to Settings → Developer Options and enable USB Debugging.
3
Plug your phone into the Chromebook via USB. When prompted on the phone, tap "Allow USB Debugging" and check "Always allow from this computer".
4
Honor/MagicOS: Pull down the notification shade and tap the USB connection notice → select File Transfer (MTP) — this activates ADB properly.
5
Click CONNECT PHONE, select your device, then tap Allow on your phone screen if prompted. Keep screen unlocked & on throughout.
